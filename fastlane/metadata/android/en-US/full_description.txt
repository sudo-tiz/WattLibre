Monitor and control compatible AFERIY, FOSSiBOT and SYDPOWER power stations from your phone over local Bluetooth. No account, cloud service, Internet permission, or Google Play services.

Features:
• View battery charge, incoming and outgoing power, and estimated runtime
• Control USB, AC, DC master, and lighting modes
• Reconnect automatically after a connection drop
• Save a custom device name on this phone
• Inspect and copy detailed diagnostics when troubleshooting

Designed for power stations using a BrightEMS-compatible Bluetooth protocol. Tested with the AFERIY P280.

Bluetooth permissions are required to find and connect to a station. Android 11 and older also require Location permission and the Location service enabled for BLE scanning; WattLibre does not access location data.
